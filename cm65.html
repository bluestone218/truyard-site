CM65 Chipper/Mulcher

Single blade to 65mm, 18 hammers on the flywheel.

The CM65 is a dual-function Chipper/Mulcher perfect for turning garden waste into nutrient-rich mulch. It handles dry or wet material — garden waste, newspapers, bamboo, manure, hedge trimmings, kitchen waste, hay, leaves, and palms. The CM65 chips most branches up to 65mm and is fitted with self-feeding action on an all-welded steel frame. Lightweight and easy to move around your property.
