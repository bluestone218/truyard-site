CM90 Honda GX270 Chipper/Mulcher

Twin blades to 90mm, 28 hammers on the flywheel.

The CM90 is a large-capacity, semi-commercial Chipper/Mulcher. Its wide chipper chute takes branches up to 90mm, and its large feed hopper takes garden waste by the armful. Inside, 28 hammers on a large flywheel mulch it quickly. It runs on a Honda GX270 with pull start, and an optional towing attachment lets you move it around your property.
