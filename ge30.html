GE30 Lawn Edger

Commercial edging — 300mm blade, three-wheel stability.

The GE30 is built for commercial work — golf courses, schools, municipalities and large residential grounds. A 300mm steel blade cuts the deepest of edges with precision, and a three-wheel design keeps it stable and easy to steer over long runs. Height levers and throttle are all reached from the operator position, so adjustments happen without stopping.
