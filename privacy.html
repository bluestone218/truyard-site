Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect through truyard.co.th and our other contact channels, why we collect it, and your rights under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019).

Who we are

TruYard is a brand of Highmark Industries Co., Ltd. We are responsible for your personal data (the "data controller").

Highmark Industries Co., Ltd.
35/39 Moo 13, Bang Na-Trat Road, KM 11.5, Bang Phli Yai Subdistrict, Bang Phli District, Samut Prakan 10540, Thailand
Email: info@truyard.co.th

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, phone number, email address and delivery address.
  • When you ask for a full tax invoice: the name and address for the invoice, the tax ID and the branch. For a person, the tax ID is the 13-digit national ID number.
  • When you pay: card payments are made on Bangkok Bank's secure payment page. We receive the payment result and reference, not your card number. To pay by bank transfer, contact us: we send you an invoice with our bank details, and you send us your transfer slip, which we keep to match your payment.
  • When you contact us by form, phone, LINE or email: your name, contact details and what you tell us, including any photos you send.
  • When you apply to become a dealer: your business details and the contact details of the people we deal with.
  • When you use the site: cookie data, such as your device, browser and the pages you visit. See our Cookie Policy.

Your name, phone number and delivery address are needed to fill an order, and your tax details to issue a full tax invoice. Everything else is up to you.

Who we share it with

We share your personal data only as needed to serve you, and each party gets only what it needs:

  • delivery companies and transport operators that deliver your order;
  • Bangkok Bank, which processes card payments;
  • our accountant and auditor, and government agencies such as the Revenue Department when the law requires;
  • engine manufacturers, when we make an engine warranty claim for you;
  • a TruYard dealer near you, when a dealer is better placed to help you. Dealers are separate businesses and look after your data under their own privacy policies;
  • the companies that build, host and maintain this website, and the services we use to run it: Google, YouTube and LINE.

Data stored outside Thailand

Some of the services we use, such as Google and LINE, may store data on servers outside Thailand. We use providers with recognised data protection safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep your personal data for 5 years after your last order or contact with us, or longer where the law requires, for example for accounting records. Once a year, we delete or make anonymous the data that has passed that time.

Cookie data is kept as set out in our Cookie Policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data, or send it to another organisation;
  • correct it or bring it up to date;
  • delete it or make it anonymous;
  • stop or limit how we use it;
  • withdraw any consent you have given. This applies from then on.

Where the law requires us to keep data, such as tax invoices, we keep it for the time the law sets.

To make a request, email info@truyard.co.th. We may ask you to confirm who you are, and we reply within 30 days.

You can also complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

Keeping it safe

Only staff who need your data can see it, and the site uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Cookies

We use cookies to run the site and, with your consent, to measure visits and ads. See our Cookie Policy. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.

Contact

For questions or requests about your personal data, email info@truyard.co.th or write to us at the address above.
