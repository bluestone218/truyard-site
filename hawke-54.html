Hawke Combo 54" Zero Turn Mower

The same conversion, six more inches of deck.

The Hawke 54 is a commercial-grade zero turn mower that converts in seconds between ride-on and walk-behind modes. In the ride-on position, you mow in comfort across open ground with the padded suspension seat. A single lock knob transforms it in seconds into a walk-behind machine — ready for slopes, wet or uneven turf, tight spots, and tree bases the ride-on position can't reach.
