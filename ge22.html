GE22 Lawn Edger

Clean, deep edges — the detail that finishes a lawn.

The GE22 cuts a precise, deep edge along paths, drives and borders — the difference between a lawn that has been mown and one that has been looked after. The 225mm blade adjusts to the depth you want, a single lever sets the height, and the machine is started and operated from behind, keeping you well clear of the blade. The handle folds down for storage and transport.
