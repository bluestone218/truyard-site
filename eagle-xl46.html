Eagle XL46 Zero Turn Mower

46 inches of cut, 22 horsepower behind it.

The Eagle XL46 is a zero turn mower designed for durability and comfort, built for acreage — real ground to cover. Powered by a reliable Loncin V-Twin LC2P77 22hp engine and a Hydro-Gear hydrostatic transmission, it features LED headlights, cup holders, and a suspension seat. The wide 46" cutting deck reduces mowing time, with 7 cutting height positions easily adjusted using a foot pedal and hand lever.
