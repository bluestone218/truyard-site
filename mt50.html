MT50 Trimmer Mower

Heavy duty, easy to handle, built for the rough.

The MT50 takes on ground a lawn mower can't — metre-high weeds, tall grass and rough overgrown corners. Large pneumatic wheels carry the machine's weight so it pushes easily over uneven ground, and the cutting head keeps grass and weeds from wrapping, so you keep working instead of stopping to clear it. It cuts with 4mm nylon cord.
