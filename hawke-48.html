Hawke Zero Turn Mower

Ride on. Convert. Walk behind.

The Hawke Turf is a commercial-grade zero turn mower that converts in seconds between ride-on and walk-behind. Its 48" cutting deck, or the optional 54" deck, handles open ground quickly in the seated position. When the ground gets difficult — sloped, wet, uneven, or tight — the operator steps off, swings the control arm back with a single lock knob, and keeps mowing as a walk-behind, in full control. No tools, no attachments to swap.
