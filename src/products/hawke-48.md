The Hawke 48 is a commercial-grade combo zero-turn mower built to work two ways. In the seated position it handles open ground quickly, and a quick turn of the control knob swings the arm into walk-behind mode for slopes, tight edges, and hard-to-reach corners — no second machine required.

A full-suspension padded seat keeps the ride-on position comfortable over long days, while the reinforced 8mm fabricated deck and genuine Ogura clutch are built for repeated commercial use rather than occasional weekend mowing.
