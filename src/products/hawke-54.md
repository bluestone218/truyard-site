The Hawke 54 is the wider-deck version of the Hawke combo mower, built for properties where ground coverage matters more than manoeuvring through tight gaps. Like the rest of the Hawke series, it converts from a seated zero-turn to a walk-behind mower with a single knob — no tools, no second machine.

Forward speeds up to 19 km/h and a reinforced fabricated deck mean it holds up under repeated commercial use, while the padded suspension seat keeps long mowing runs comfortable.
