Eagle Series Zero Turn Mowers

Built for the long mowing day.

The Eagle Series is built for acreage — real ground to cover, from tighter properties up to bigger jobs that need more deck and more power. All three models share the comfort features that make a long mowing day easier — suspension seats, cup holders, LED headlights. The XL46 and XL50 share the same Loncin engine across a 46" or 50" deck; the XK50 steps up to a Kawasaki engine on the same 50" deck. All three are built on the same all-steel tubular frame and fabricated, reinforced steel deck.
