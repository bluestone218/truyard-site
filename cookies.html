Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page explains the cookies truyard.co.th uses and how you choose which ones to allow. The site is run by Highmark Industries Co., Ltd.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. They let the site remember things, such as what is in your cart, and show us how the site is used.

The cookies we use

TypeWhat they doSet byHow longYour choice
Strictly necessaryKeep the site working: your cart, checkout, language, security and your cookie choiceOur websiteMost until you close your browser; your cookie choice for 12 monthsAlways on, as the site needs them to work
AnalyticsCount visits and show which pages people use, so we can improve the siteGoogle AnalyticsUp to 2 yearsOnly with your consent
AdvertisingShow whether a visit came from one of our Google ads, so we know which ads workGoogle AdsUp to 90 daysOnly with your consent
VideoPlay our product videos, which come from YouTubeYouTube (Google)Set by YouTube, under Google's privacy policyOnly with your consent

Your choice

When you first visit, a banner asks you to choose:

  • Necessary only: the site uses strictly necessary cookies only.
  • Accept all: the site also uses analytics, advertising and video cookies.

Analytics, advertising and video cookies stay off until you choose. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

Other websites

When you pay by card, you move to Bangkok Bank's payment page, which uses its own cookies under Bangkok Bank's policy. Our LINE button and YouTube links take you to those services, which have their own cookie and privacy policies.

More information

For how we handle your personal data, see our Privacy Policy. For questions, email info@truyard.co.th.
