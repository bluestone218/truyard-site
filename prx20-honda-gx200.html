PRX20 Honda GX200 Wood Chipper

Designed for palms. Large hopper, faster processing.

A wood chipper with a large feed hopper, making feeding easier and material processing faster. Designed to process fibrous material including palms and banana palms. Its chipper blade is high carbon steel, and it is fitted with pneumatic wheels. Chips most branches up to 65mm.
